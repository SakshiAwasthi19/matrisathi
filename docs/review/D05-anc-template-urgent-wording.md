MatriSathi records and reminds; it never decides. It does not diagnose, score risk, interpret results or give medical advice. Everything clinical that the app shows comes from what you approve here, word for word, with your name and the date.

Until you approve a template, the app uses a **placeholder** with no clinical content, labelled on every screen: "SYNTHETIC PLACEHOLDER – NOT CLINICALLY APPROVED". It is never used with real patients.

**How the app will use this table**
- When a clinician publishes a plan for a mother, the app creates her appointments and tasks from these rows.
- Items whose due time has already passed when she first arrives are **not** created as missed visits; the app never invents a past visit. (Section 6 asks what you want instead.)
- If the expected date is unknown, items timed by pregnancy week are **not** scheduled until a date is recorded. Items timed "at first contact" are.
- When a clinician corrects the date, only future, not-yet-attended items move. Past and completed items stay as they were. Every change is recorded with who made it and when.
- "Overdue" in the app means an administrative delay (the task was not done on time), never a clinical warning.
